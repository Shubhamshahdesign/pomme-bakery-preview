One baker, everything from scratch

Pomme opened on Kingsland Road in August 2023. Ivie Egonmwan, who owns it, bakes every pastry herself, and every recipe on the counter is her own.

She uses sustainable ingredients and buys from local, independent and small-scale suppliers wherever she can. Expect a dark, glossy bake on the croissants and flavours you won't find in other London bakeries, from twice-baked coconut and pistachio pain au chocolat to quince tarte tatin in the autumn.

“With precision, creativity and some of the best ingredients available.”

Palate Magazine, on Ivie's pastries
